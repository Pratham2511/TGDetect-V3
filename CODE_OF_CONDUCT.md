# Code of Conduct

We want TGDetect discussions and contributions to be welcoming and useful. Participants should treat one another respectfully, give constructive technical feedback, and make room for different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Expected behavior includes listening to feedback, discussing ideas without personal attacks, respecting privacy, and helping keep reports reproducible and focused. Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing, and sharing someone else's private information are unacceptable.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces representing this project. Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close a discussion, or restrict participation when needed, with responses proportionate to the behavior.

Report concerns privately to the repository owner through the [contact channels on their GitHub profile](https://github.com/Pratham2511), where available. Do not post private details or identify affected people publicly. Maintainers should handle reports discreetly and address concerns without retaliation.
